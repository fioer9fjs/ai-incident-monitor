# AI Company Faces Lawsuit Over Alleged Training on Pirated Content

An AI company is facing a massive lawsuit for allegedly training its models using pirated content from platforms like Library Genesis and Pirate Library Mirror. The legal action, involving major publishers and Sony Music, highlights critical intellectual property disputes in AI development. This case emphasizes the escalating legal risks associated with unauthorized data scraping for generative AI.
