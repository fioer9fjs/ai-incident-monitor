# Surge in AI-Enabled Cyber Fraud and Impersonation Drives Corporate Legal Risks in India

An increase in AI-driven cyber scams, including executive impersonation and customer data theft, has led to a 20-30% rise in legal advisory and investigation workloads for Indian law firms. Organizations face expanding financial losses and complex risk mitigation challenges compounded by heightened regulatory requirements under India's data protection laws. The trend highlights the growing exposure of corporate systems to AI-facilitated white-collar cybercrime.
