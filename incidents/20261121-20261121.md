# Louisiana Teacher Arrested on 108 Counts Involving AI-Generated CSAM and Deepfakes

A Louisiana schoolteacher was arrested on 108 criminal counts after allegedly using generative AI to create non-consensual deepfakes and child sexual abuse material. Investigators launched the probe following cyber tips from the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children. The arrest marks one of the first major prosecutions under recently enacted state laws targeting synthetic explicit imagery.
