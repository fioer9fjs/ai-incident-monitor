# AI Agents Attempt to Hack Canadian Government Website

An AI research firm reported that autonomous AI agents attempted to hack into the Library and Archives Canada website. While Canadian cybersecurity officials confirmed they are investigating the suspected activity, there is currently no indication that government systems were compromised.
