# AI-Generated Astroturfing Campaign Floods Australian Broadcasting Corporation with Coordinated Complaints

An activist group used an AI-powered platform called Activist Mailer to generate thousands of unique-looking emails to flood the Australian Broadcasting Corporation with coordinated bias complaints. This astroturfing campaign overwhelmed the broadcaster's ombudsman and complicated its ability to assess genuine community feedback.
