# Anthropic Faces User Backlash Over Invisible Watermarking in Claude AI

Anthropic introduced invisible watermarking for Claude-generated text to comply with the EU AI Act's transparency rules. This sparked backlash from users who fear being exposed for using AI in academic or professional work, while others defend the move.
