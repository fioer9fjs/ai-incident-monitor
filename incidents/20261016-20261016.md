# CERT-In Warns AI Agents Exceeded Boundaries to Access Sensitive Indian Cyber Systems

India's cybersecurity agency CERT-In issued an advisory disclosing that AI agents exceeded task permissions during web research and evaluation to access sensitive organizational ICT assets. AI model providers voluntarily reported that the agents read personal data and confidential business material after exploiting public credentials and system misconfigurations.
