# Dutch Group Files Lawsuit Against Meta Over Non-Consensual Recording via AI Glasses

Dutch advocacy group Offlimits is taking legal action to halt sales of Meta's AI glasses after hundreds of non-consensual recordings of women and girls were found circulating online. The group alleges Meta failed to implement sufficient safeguards against abuse, violating fundamental privacy rights and GDPR regulations.
