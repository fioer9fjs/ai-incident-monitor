# Study Finds Popular AI Chatbots Recommend Scam E-Commerce Websites to Shoppers

A study by intellectual property firm Podqi revealed that popular AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google Gemini frequently link users to fraudulent e-commerce sites. Nearly 9% of analyzed shopping queries contained references to dangerous portals or counterfeit sellers, exposing consumers to digital scams. OpenAI and Google acknowledged the findings and noted ongoing efforts to improve safeguards.
