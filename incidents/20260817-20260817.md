# Claude AI Outage Due to Authentication Service Failure

Anthropic's Claude AI services, including the chatbot, API, and developer tools, experienced a brief widespread outage due to an authentication service failure. The issue affected over 15,000 users in the United States before being resolved by the company. The root cause of the disruption remains unannounced.
