# Vietnamese Police Warn of AI Deepfake and DeepVoice Impersonation Scams

Vietnamese authorities have warned the public about 25 cyber-scam tactics, highlighting the increasing use of AI, Deepfake, and DeepVoice technologies. Criminals use these tools to clone voices and faces of acquaintances or officials to deceive victims into transferring money.
