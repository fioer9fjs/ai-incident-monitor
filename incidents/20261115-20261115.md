# Nationwide Air Traffic Control Upgrade Failure Causes Four-Hour US Ground Stop

A major technology upgrade overloaded air traffic control equipment, causing a nationwide four-hour ground stop across the United States alongside widespread Microsoft service disruptions. Public anxiety fueled speculation of cyberattacks or AI chaos, though officials attributed the incidents to routine system upgrade failures.
