# Babylon Bee Challenges New Mexico AI Disclosure Law over Satire Rights

The Babylon Bee has sued New Mexico over a law requiring disclosures on AI-generated political media, claiming it violates First Amendment protections for satire. The lawsuit centers on an AI-generated parody video of a state Supreme Court Justice. The case highlights the tension between combating deceptive deepfakes and protecting free speech.
