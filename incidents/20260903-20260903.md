# NYC Public Schools Ban Generative AI for Students Below High School

New York City has implemented a temporary one-year ban on student-facing generative AI and chatbots for students below the eighth grade. The suspension affects approximately 600,000 students and is intended to allow school administrators to assess the risks and establish proper guidelines.
