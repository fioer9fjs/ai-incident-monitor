# Meta Settles Lawsuit Over Addictive Teen Recommendation Algorithms

Meta has settled a lawsuit addressing allegations that its social media recommendation algorithms intentionally fostered addiction among teenage users. The case highlights the mental health risks associated with engagement-maximizing algorithms. This settlement represents a significant step in holding tech companies liable for algorithmic harms.
