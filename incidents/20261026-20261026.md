# Meta Delays Updated Ad Terms Following EU Antitrust Complaints Over AI Content Scraping

Meta postponed enforcing revised commercial terms after European publishers and regulators raised concerns over non-consensual AI data harvesting. Publishers allege the terms grant Meta blanket rights to train AI models on their web archives without explicit consent.
