# South Australia Mandates Ban on AI-Generated Ticketless Parking Fines

South Australia's premier has instructed local councils to stop using AI-equipped camera cars to generate ticketless parking fines. The policy intervention addresses procedural fairness and transparency concerns surrounding automated enforcement systems.
