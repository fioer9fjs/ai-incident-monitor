# DraftKings Class Action Alleges Predatory AI Targeting of Losing Gamblers

A federal class-action lawsuit accuses DraftKings of using a machine-learning model to identify and target losing gamblers with promotions to encourage continued betting. The lawsuit alleges this predatory practice violates Massachusetts regulations and the company's own responsible gambling commitments. DraftKings denies the allegations.
