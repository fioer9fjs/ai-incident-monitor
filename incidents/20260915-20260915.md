# SEC Data Center Contractor Settles Allegations of Fraudulent Tier III Certification

AiNET Corp and its former CEO settled allegations of defrauding the SEC by fabricating a fake certification body to pass off their data center as Tier III compliant. The SEC experienced security, cooling, and power issues at the facility before terminating the contract. The case highlights critical infrastructure verification risks.
