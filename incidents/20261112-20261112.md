# UK Police Watchdog Warns AI Facial Recognition Bias Risks Wrongful Arrests

A report by the UK police watchdog HMICFRS warned that law enforcement risks wrongly identifying and arresting innocent people, particularly from ethnic minorities, due to biased AI facial recognition systems. Inspectors found officers frequently treated AI-generated matches as evidence rather than intelligence and highlighted a critical lack of legal safeguards.
