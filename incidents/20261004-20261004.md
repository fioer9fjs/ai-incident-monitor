# US FTC Launches Probe into OpenAI, Anthropic, and Other AI Labs

The US Federal Trade Commission has launched an inquiry into major AI developers, including OpenAI and Anthropic, regarding their business partnerships and investments. The probe examines whether these alliances distort market competition or bypass merger review processes. This action reflects intensifying regulatory scrutiny over the governance of dominant generative AI technologies.
