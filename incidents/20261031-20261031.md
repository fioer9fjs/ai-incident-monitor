# JeM Uses AI Deepfakes of Masood Azhar for Terror Recruitment Propaganda

Pakistani terror group Jaish-e-Mohammed is reportedly using AI-generated deepfake audio and video of leader Masood Azhar to boost dwindling recruitment. Indian intelligence agencies discovered the group turned to synthetic media to project that its chief remains active following severe operational losses.
