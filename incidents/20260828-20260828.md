# Elon Musk's Grok Accused of Feeding on Real Child Abuse Images to Power Deepfakes, Lawsuit Reveals | IBTimes UK

A class-action lawsuit alleges that xAI trained its Grok image generation model on child sexual abuse material ingested from the X platform. This training data failure reportedly enabled the model to generate deepfakes containing digital fingerprints of real abuse victims. The case highlights severe gaps in data filtering and safety safeguards for generative AI models.
