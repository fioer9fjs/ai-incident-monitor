# MI5 Chief Warns of AI Security Threats Including Cyber Attacks, Propaganda, and Bioweapons

MI5 Director General Ken McCallum warned that artificial intelligence is accelerating national security risks facing the UK. He highlighted concerns over threat actors using AI to supercharge cyber warfare, create targeted propaganda, and potentially assist in developing biological weapons.
