# IIA Nigeria Warns of AI-Driven Vulnerabilities and Cyber Fraud Risks

The Institute of Internal Auditors Nigeria has warned that organizations face increased risks of financial fraud and data breaches due to the rapid adoption of AI without adequate controls. The institute called for stronger internal auditing practices and regulatory frameworks to mitigate these emerging digital threats.
