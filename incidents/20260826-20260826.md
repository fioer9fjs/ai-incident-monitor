# Apple Seeks Expedited Discovery in OpenAI Trade Secret Lawsuit

Apple has filed a reply to expedite discovery in its lawsuit accusing former employees and OpenAI of trade secret misappropriation. Apple alleges that OpenAI is actively embedding stolen proprietary technology into its hardware development efforts. OpenAI has opposed the motion, claiming the request is overbroad and aimed at spying on its competitive strategies.
