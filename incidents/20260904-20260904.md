# AI-Generated 'Cat in the Hat' Images Used to Threaten Mississippi Schools

An individual used AI-generated images of the 'Cat in the Hat' to post targeted threats against students and schools in Lamar County, Mississippi. The posts triggered a multi-agency investigation involving the FBI, causing widespread panic and disruption within the community.
