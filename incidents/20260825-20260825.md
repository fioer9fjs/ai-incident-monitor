# Bermuda Police Warn of AI Deepfake and WhatsApp Impersonation Scams

The Bermuda Police Service issued an official advisory warning the public about scammers using AI deepfakes and voice cloning to impersonate trusted individuals. These tactics are combined with compromised WhatsApp accounts to deceive victims into sending money. The incident highlights the growing threat of generative AI being weaponized for financial fraud.
