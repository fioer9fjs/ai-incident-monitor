# Man Sues ByteDance After Doubao AI Recommends Inauspicious Burial Date

A man in China sued ByteDance after its Doubao AI chatbot recommended a burial date that it later claimed was inauspicious, leading his family to blame the AI for a relative's subsequent car accident. The user suffered severe psychological distress and used the AI itself to draft the lawsuit. Legal experts suggest the case is unlikely to succeed due to a lack of direct causation.
