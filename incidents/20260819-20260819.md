# Securities Class Action Lawsuit Filed Against Datavault AI Inc.

Datavault AI Inc. is facing a securities class action lawsuit alleging misleading statements regarding its business operations and AI technology. The lawsuit, announced by Levi & Korsinsky, LLP, seeks to recover damages for investors who suffered financial losses. This highlights ongoing scrutiny over corporate governance and potential 'AI washing' in public markets.
