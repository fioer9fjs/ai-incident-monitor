# Demographic Bias and Missing Ancestry Data Discovered in Human Cell Atlas AI Training Dataset

An analysis of the Human Cell Atlas, a key reference dataset used to train medical AI, revealed that nearly 70% of samples lack ancestry data, while European ancestries are overrepresented six-fold. This severe demographic bias risks training medical AI models that perform poorly or inaccurately for non-European populations.
