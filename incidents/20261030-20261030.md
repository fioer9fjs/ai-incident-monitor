# Mistral Releases Open-Weight 'Mistral Large 4' Model Despite Cyberattack Capability Warnings

Mistral AI has released the open weights for its trillion-parameter Mistral Large 4 model despite acknowledging its potential to assist in real cyberattacks. Because open weights can be run locally without external oversight, safeguards built into the download cannot be dynamically updated post-release. Experts warn that open-sourcing highly capable dual-use models creates persistent cybersecurity risks.
