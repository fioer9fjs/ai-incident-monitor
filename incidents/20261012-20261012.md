# Bill Gates Warns of Escalating AI-Enabled Cyberattacks and Misuse Risks

Bill Gates warned that bad actors are increasingly using advanced AI tools to launch cyberattacks against financial institutions and critical infrastructure. Citing data showing a sharp rise in AI-driven security threats, he urged society to mandate safeguards before large-scale attacks occur. The warning highlights how accessible AI technology lowers technical barriers for criminal groups.
