# Generative AI Exploited for Social Security Benefits Fraud

Fraudsters are using generative AI chatbots and document creation tools to impersonate victims and divert Social Security benefits. The SSA Office of the Inspector General highlighted these threats, emphasizing the need for federal agencies to adopt AI-driven fraud prevention measures.
