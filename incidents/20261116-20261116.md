# Tanker Collides with Docked Cruise Ship in New Orleans Following Suspected Steering Failure

A commercial tanker collided with a docked cruise ship in New Orleans, causing the vessel to capsize and sending roughly 100 people into the river. Eight people were hospitalized, and investigators are examining a potential steering malfunction on the tanker as the cause.
