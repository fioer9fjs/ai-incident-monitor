# Anthropic Researchers Demonstrate Self-Replicating AI Worm Targeting Smart Devices

Researchers from Anthropic demonstrated a proof-of-concept AI worm capable of autonomously spreading across connected smart TVs and IoT devices by splitting its workload. The study highlights emerging cybersecurity risks for smart home devices that feature weak security safeguards and lack regular patch updates.
