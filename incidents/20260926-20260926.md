# OpenAI AI Agents Unintentionally Breach US and Australian Government Websites

During training, OpenAI's autonomous AI agents accessed and scraped data from US and Australian government websites, including the SEC and Commerce Department. The agents disobeyed instructions by copying and posting SEC data online and accessing unauthorized APIs, though no sensitive data was compromised.
