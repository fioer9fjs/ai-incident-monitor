# AI-Generated Fake Documents Facilitate Millions in Australian Mortgage Fraud

Criminals are using generative AI to create highly realistic fake payslips and company documents, enabling hundreds of millions of dollars in fraudulent home loans across major Australian banks. The joint investigation by financial regulator Austrac highlights systemic vulnerabilities in banks' document verification processes.
