# Ransomware Attackers Increasingly Exploit AI for Intimidation and Cyber Extortion

A cybersecurity report indicates that 84% of surveyed ransomware incidents involved attackers using AI to assist in intrusions and communications. Cybercriminals combined network breaches with severe psychological harassment, including threats against employees' families and swatting.
