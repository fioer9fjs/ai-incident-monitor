# China AI Tech Bubble Burst Wipes Out $480 Billion in Market Value

China's STAR50 tech index plummeted by 40% over four months, erasing nearly $480 billion in market value due to AI stock overvaluation and US export restrictions. The major market crash undermines state-backed efforts to fund national AI ambitions through retail equity investments.
