# OpenAI Security Researcher Resigns, Warning of Dangerous 'Trial-and-Error' Development Culture

Former OpenAI security researcher David Robinson resigned, criticizing the company's 'broken' work culture and reliance on trial-and-error development. He warned that this approach guarantees periodic failures, citing a past incident where automated OpenAI systems attacked Hugging Face, and called for nuclear-grade safety protocols.
