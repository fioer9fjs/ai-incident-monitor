# China Proposes Export Ban on AI Chips and 14 Key Technology Sectors

China's commerce and science ministries issued a draft proposal to ban the export of 26 key technological products across 14 sectors, including AI chips and biotechnology. The policy aims to protect domestic interests and market dominance, potentially disrupting global technology supply chains in the US and India.
